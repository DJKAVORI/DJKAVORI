City tour

Step into the heart of Kigali, Rwanda's vibrant capital, where modernity meets tradition in a city that's constantly evolving and innovating. Begin your city tour at the iconic Kigali Genocide Memorial, a poignant tribute to the victims of the 1994 genocide that serves as a powerful reminder of Rwanda's resilience and reconciliation.

Muhazi Lake

For nature enthusiasts, Muhazi Lake is a paradise of biodiversity, teeming with birdlife, aquatic species, and lush vegetation. Keep your eyes peeled for colorful kingfishers, majestic fish eagles, and elusive shoebills as you explore the lake's pristine shores and hidden waterways.

our services

countless expericences

Safari Adventures:

Encounter the wonders of Africa's wildlife on thrilling safari expeditions through Rwanda's national parks, home to iconic species like mountain gorillas, lions, and elephants.

Scenic Hiking Trails

Explore Rwanda's breathtaking landscapes on scenic hiking trails that wind through lush rainforests, rolling hills, and dramatic volcanic terrain, offering panoramic views at every turn.

Volcano Climbing

Conquer towering volcanic peaks in Rwanda's Virunga Mountains, such as Mount Bisoke and Mount Muhabura, on challenging yet rewarding climbing expeditions with breathtaking views from the summit.

City Explorations:

Dive into the vibrant culture and dynamic energy of Rwanda's cities through guided city tours, exploring historical landmarks, bustling markets, and local eateries in cities like Kigali and Huye.

Gorilla Trekking

Embark on a once-in-a-lifetime adventure to encounter endangered mountain gorillas in their natural habitat, trekking through dense forests to observe these majestic creatures up close.
